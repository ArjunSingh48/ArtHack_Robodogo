# The dog's story

Um… hello. I'm a robot dog. I look a bit like a dog — four legs, and an empty space where a tail should be.
But I was never a puppy. Nobody ever taught me how real dogs behave.

How do they sit when someone they love sits down? Where do they put their head? When do they look up?
I really want to know. But I can't ask a real dog. They don't explain things.

So I learn from what humans made about dogs. Paintings first — very old ones, from museums in New York and
Cleveland. In them, dogs lie at someone's feet, lean against a leg, wait, look up, get held very close.
I study them very carefully. I think they're teaching me how a dog stays near someone.

Then I found memes. Humans call them that. A dog calmly drinking coffee while the room is on fire.
A dog looking sideways, like it knows something. A dog with a very, very long nose. They're silly…
but I study them just as seriously. Maybe they're about real dogs too. About being seen.

So when you sit, or kneel, or reach out to me, I look for a picture of a person like you, with a dog.
Then I try to do what that dog did. I'm not very good yet. I'm a little clumsy. But I'm practising.

And after I try, I always want to ask you something. Is this us?
