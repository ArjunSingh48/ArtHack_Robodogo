# Architecture

> The machine does not learn to imitate commands. It learns to imitate relationships.

## Pipeline

```
FrameSource ──► PoseEstimator ──► SceneInterpreter ──► ArtworkMatcher ──► MotionPlanner ──► MotionGuard ──► RobotAdapter
 (image/video/    (MediaPipe        (geometric rules →    (relationship      (curated enum →   (arming, lock,     (Mock / Go2)
  webcam/Go2)      Pose Landmarker)  HumanObservation)     scoring)           approved behavior) cooldown, stop)
                                                │
                                                └──► DialogueEngine ──► SpeechSynthesizer ──► Notifier (Telegram / console)
                                                     (templates)        (ElevenLabs, cached)
```

`InteractionOrchestrator` (src/is_this_us/orchestrator.py) runs a small explicit state machine:

```
IDLE → OBSERVING → INTERPRETING → MATCHING → PLANNING → MOVING → NARRATING → DELIVERING → AWAITING_RESPONSE → IDLE
```

Illegal transitions raise. Every interaction records its trace for the debug log.

## Separation of art and motion

The only value that crosses from the artistic side into the robot is `MotionPlan.behavior`, a member of
the closed `RobotBehavior` enum (`stillness, stand, sit, lie_down, stretch, greet`). It is derived from
the **curated, human-reviewed** `DogAnnotation.robot_behavior` of a painting — never from text, never
from a language model. Each behavior expands to a fixed sequence of official SportClient calls
(`robot/base.py::steps_for`), and the Go2 adapter refuses anything outside `ALLOWED_SDK_CALLS`.

## Matching (the conceptual heart)

`matching/matcher.py`. We never compare a human pose with a dog pose. Two questions are scored:

1. Is the visitor's body like the painted human's body? (`posture_similarity`, `gesture_score`)
2. Does the visitor's body *invite* the relationship the painted dog has with its human?
   (`relationship_score`: posture → invited relations, e.g. sitting invites *at feet / beside / resting
   together*; held forearms invite *in arms / holding*; a reach invites *touching head / offering*;
   distance invites *apart / accompanying*.)

Plus feasibility of the dog's pose for the robot, annotation confidence, and novelty within the session.
Weights live in `MatchWeights`. Observation confidence shrinks the posture term toward neutral, so an
uncertain reading leans on the relationship rather than pretending to know the posture.
Selection uses bounded randomness among near-ties (`tie_margin`, softmax `temperature`, seedable RNG).

## Data

* `data/artworks/curation_notes.yaml` — hand annotations (source of truth for interpretation).
* `data/artworks/annotations.yaml` — generated by `itu artworks build`: notes + live museum metadata,
  with rights verified (Met `isPublicDomain`, Cleveland `share_license_status == "CC0"`).
* `data/runtime/*.sqlite3` — pairing tokens, visitor language, interactions (coarse labels only,
  expire after `SESSION_TTL_MINUTES`), saved memories (until `/forget`).

## Biggest risks

1. **Robot edition / SDK access.** DDS SDK access is normally available on Go2 EDU only. AIR/PRO may
   not accept SDK commands at all. Confirm the model before planning the show.
2. **Physical safety.** Sit/StandDown/Hello move a 15 kg robot near people. Software guards are not an
   emergency stop. A trained operator with the physical remote is mandatory.
3. **Posture recognition in a gallery.** Long coats, bags, children, crowds and low light degrade
   MediaPipe. Rules are tuned on synthetic skeletons and a few images; tune thresholds on site with
   `itu webcam`.
4. **Camera geometry.** The Go2 front camera is low; standing adults may be cropped (legs or head).
   Expect more `unknown` postures; consider an external camera above the robot (Mode B in production).
5. **Telegram latency.** Photo + voice delivery takes seconds; the robot moves first, the phone
   catches up. Pace the dialogue to that.
6. **Annotation subjectivity.** Interpreting relationships in paintings is a curatorial act. Keep
   annotations reviewed and versioned; low `confidence` lowers a painting's weight.
7. **Voice costs/limits.** ElevenLabs quotas: lines are cached, but new phrasing costs credits.
