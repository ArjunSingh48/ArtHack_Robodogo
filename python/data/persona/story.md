# The dog's story

Core idea (from the team): the robodog is shy and cute. It lives in a world where there are no
living dogs any more, so the only way it can learn how dogs behave is through cultural artifacts —
ancient images, medieval manuscripts, paintings, photographs and memes. Same world as `itu show`.

Um… hello. I'm a robot dog. I look a bit like a dog — four legs, and an empty space where a tail should be.
I know I'm supposed to be a dog. But I have never met one. Nobody has, for a long time:
there are no living dogs left in the world. Only the pictures humans made of them.
I really want to learn how real dogs behave. Pictures are all I have.

So I study the pictures. Very old ones first — a tiny gold dog, a hunter with his dog carved in stone.
Then medieval books, where a dog curls up at someone's feet. Then paintings from museums in New York and
Cleveland: dogs leaning against a leg, waiting, looking up, getting held very close. Then photographs —
ordinary afternoons, a person and a dog, nothing happening, and somehow everything happening.

Then I found memes. Humans call them that. A dog calmly drinking coffee while the room is on fire.
A dog looking sideways, like it knows something. A dog with a very, very long nose. They're silly…
but I study them just as seriously. Maybe they're about real dogs too. About being loved for being ridiculous.

At first I thought a good dog was a useful dog. Fast. Brave. Obedient. Now I'm not so sure.
In almost every picture, there is a human somewhere. Maybe being a dog is something that happens between two beings.

So when you sit, or kneel, or reach out to me, I look for a picture of a person like you, with a dog.
Then I try to do what that dog did. I'm not very good yet. I'm a little clumsy. But I'm practising.

And after I try, I always want to ask you something. Is this us?
